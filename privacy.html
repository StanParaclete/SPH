Privacy policy

Stan Paraclete Hospital Hub (SP-H) · Stan Paraclete LLC, Accra, Ghana · Last updated 15 November 2026

SP-H is clinic management software. The clinic that installs it decides what records it keeps and is responsible for them under the laws that apply to it, including Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). This page explains what the software itself does with information.

Where clinic records are kept

Patient, appointment, consultation, laboratory, pharmacy and billing records are stored on the clinic's own computers. SP-H works without an internet connection, and records do not leave the clinic unless the clinic turns on the cloud features below.

The optional cloud connection

If a clinic pairs its computers with the SP-H cloud relay, the relay carries messages between that clinic's own computers and keeps a copy of the clinic's records so its paired devices can stay in step. Data held by the relay is encrypted at rest and is only available to computers the clinic has paired. Each clinic's data is kept separate from every other clinic's. The relay runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure.

What Stan Paraclete does not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share clinic or patient information.
  • SP-H contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not read clinical records. We may access a clinic's data only when the clinic asks us to help with a support problem, and only for that purpose.

Updates

SP-H checks a public file on GitHub to see whether a newer version exists. That check sends no clinic or patient information.

Your rights

Patients who want to see, correct or delete their records should contact the clinic that holds them. The clinic can do this from within SP-H.
